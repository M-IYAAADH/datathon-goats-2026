# Team lead declaration

Project: Maldives Island Journey Planner (Datathon 2026)
Repository: https://github.com/M-IYAAADH/datathon-goats-2026
Data repository: https://github.com/M-IYAAADH/datathon-2026

## Data sources
- MTCC 2026 ferry timetables (published PDFs; extracted tables).
- FollowMe vessel GPS, August 2025, supplied by the organisers. Only disclosure-filtered aggregates (inferred services with device and day counts at or above 3 devices / 8 days) are used in the app.
- Census 2022 per-island extract (population, age bands, disability, elderly living alone, island coordinates).

## AI tools used
Claude / Claude Code, Codex / ChatGPT and Gemini. What each did and how outputs were checked is in `docs/ai_use.md`.

## Statements
- No personal data is included in this repository or the app bundle: no device IDs, IMEIs, raw GPS tracks or individual positions.
- No API keys, passwords or other secrets are included.
- Inferred services are estimates, labelled as such in the app. Cancellations are simulated and shift proposals are recommendations only.

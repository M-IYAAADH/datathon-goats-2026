Notes

  • Stops with a parent_station are merged into that island. Otherwise each stop_id is treated as one island harbour, and islands.csv and crossings.csv must use the same IDs.
  • routes.txt takes extra columns: service_class (ferry, speedboat_verified or speedboat_unverified), source, source_date, uncertainty. Unverified speedboats are excluded from all calculations.
  • Route lines are drawn straight between consecutive stops (shapes.txt is not used).
  • Minimum transfer time is 10 min. A transfer counts as a connection when the wait is 10–120 min.
